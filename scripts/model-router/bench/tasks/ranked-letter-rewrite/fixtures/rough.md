It is with tremendous enthusiasm that we highlight our fantastic quarter, though honestly we are not sure the trend continues. The synthetic portfolio gained 4% in Q2, net of fees, compared with 3% for its stated benchmark. Most of the gain came from an unrealized recovery in two industrial holdings, not from sold positions. We sold a small consumer holding at a realized loss of 0.6% of starting portfolio value. Demand at the industrial businesses is still uneven, and their debt refinancing next year could reduce the recovery. We are confident but not actually forecasting another 4% next quarter. Cash was 12% at quarter end, up from 8%, because sale proceeds have not yet been reinvested. In summary we think there are opportunities and uncertainty and opportunities.
