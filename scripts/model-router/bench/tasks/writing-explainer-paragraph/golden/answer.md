A drawdown measures how far a portfolio has fallen from its best point. A portfolio is simply a collection of investments. To find the drawdown, take the highest value the portfolio reached, find the lowest value it fell to afterwards, and state the fall as a percentage of that high. Suppose a portfolio reached 100 and later dropped to 80. The fall is 20, and 20 divided by the high of 100 is 20%, so the drawdown was 20%. Keep one limit in mind: a drawdown describes a decline that has already happened. It does not predict the next one.
